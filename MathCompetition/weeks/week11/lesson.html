⚡
A view shows the tallest stack in each line of sight.STACK PLAN = look straight down, every square shows how many cubes are stacked there. Surface area & volume

Part 2. Boxes and glued cubes · scaling · volume.

⚡
Add every face, then take away the hidden ones.A box has 6 faces in 3 matching pairs: top & bottom, front & back, left & right. SA = 2(l×w + l×h + w×h). A cube with edge s: SA = 6 × s². Surface area uses square units (cm²); volume = l × w × h uses cubic units (cm³).

🦊
Slide the sides of the box. See the three pairs of faces and how they add up. Switch on the 1 cm squares to count them.

The point to land

Every 3D problem becomes easy once you break it into flat pieces you can count.

  • Views → the tallest stack in each line of sight
  • Surface area → every face, minus the hidden ones
  • Scaling → length × k, area × k², volume × k³
