Word Problems: find ONE first

Four moves that turn a long word problem into a few easy steps. Play with each one — then the homework is yours.

🚜RatesFind the amount for ONE, then scale. 🐔Two kindsPretend they’re all one kind, then swap. 🛒Compare cartsMake two carts match. The difference is ONE item. 🕵️What’s missing?List all the possibilities first.

World 1

Rates

Share a big amount equally, then ask how long one share lasts.

⚡
Find the amount for ONE.One bag, one day, one item. Then it is just ÷ or ×.
🛍️ Explore · The share-out machine
🦊
A machine shares rice equally into bags. Change the amount and the number of bags — how much goes into ONE bag? Then tap how much a family eats a day.
📜 Gauss 2018 · Grade 7 · Q13Medium

🌾 A factory shares 11 410 kg of rice equally into 3260 bags. A family eats 0.25 kg a day. How many days does one bag last?

Show the exact contest wording
At a factory, 11 410 kg of rice is distributed equally into 3260 bags. A family uses 0.25 kg of rice each day. How many days would it take this family to use up one bag of rice?
(A) 9(B) 12(C) 13(D) 14(E) 15
🐔
World 2

Two kinds, one total

Some are one kind, the rest are another. You know the total. How many of each?

⚡
Pretend they are ALL one kind.Then swap one at a time: each swap changes the total by the same amount.
🐄 Explore · The swap lab
🦊
A farm has 10 animals: ducks (2 legs) and cows (4 legs). Slide the number of cows and watch the legs. Start with all ducks!
📜 Gauss 2018 · Grade 7 · Q15Medium

🐦 Karl has 30 birds: emus and chickens. He gives out 100 treats: 2 for each emu, 4 for each chicken. How many chickens?

Show the exact contest wording
Karl has 30 birds. Some of his birds are emus and the rest are chickens. Karl hands out 100 treats to his birds. Each emu gets 2 treats and each chicken gets 4 treats. How many chickens does Karl have?
(A) 10(B) 15(C) 25(D) 20(E) 6
🛒
World 3

Compare the carts

Two shopping carts, two totals. Make the carts look alike — then the difference is just ONE item.

⚡
Line up the carts, then subtract.If a cart doesn’t match, double or triple it first.
✏️ Explore · Pens and notebooks
🦊
Cart A: 1 pen + 1 notebook = $5. Cart B: 1 pen + 2 notebooks = $8. Guess the pen price and see if cart B comes out right. Or compare the carts and skip the guessing!
📜 Gauss 2016 · Grade 8 · Q14Medium

⚽ 1 ball + 1 shirt = $100. 2 balls + 3 shirts = $262. What does one ball cost?

Show the exact contest wording
One soccer ball and one soccer shirt together cost $100. Two soccer balls and three soccer shirts together cost $262. What is the cost of one soccer ball?
(A) $38(B) $50(C) $87.30(D) $45(E) $40

World 4 · optional challenge

What’s missing?

Sometimes the question isn’t “what’s the answer?” but “what one more fact would let us find it?”

⚡
List ALL the possibilities first.The missing clue is the fact that splits them apart.
📜 CJMC (Gray Jay) 2021 · Q11Hard

🥪 A vending machine starts with 13 chicken ($3), 14 cheese ($3) and 15 egg ($2.50) sandwiches. It made $61, of which $21 was cheese. Which extra fact lets us find how many of each type are left?

Show the exact contest wording
At the start of the day a vending machine had 42 sandwiches in it: 13 chicken sandwiches at $3 each, 14 cheese sandwiches at $3 each, 15 egg sandwiches at $2.50 each. At the end of the day the total money made was $61, of which $21 came from cheese sandwich sales. What additional piece of information would allow us to solve the question “How many sandwiches of each type were left at the end of the day?”
(A) Total price of remaining sandwiches.(B) Number of remaining sandwiches.(C) Total price of sandwiches at the beginning of the day.(D) Number of remaining cheese sandwiches.(E) No additional information is needed.
The point to land

Long word problems become short when you pick the right move.

  • Rate → the amount for ONE
  • Two kinds → all one kind, then swap
  • Two carts → match them, subtract
  • Missing clue → list every possibility
