World 1

Multiples & the PPCM

PPCM = Plus Petit Commun Multiple — the first time two lists of multiples meet.

⚡
PPCM = “meet again”.Same time? At once? Together again? Think PPCM.
🔔 Explore · The beep race
🦊
Each clock beeps every so many minutes. They all start together at 0. Change the intervals and watch for the gold line — the first time all clocks beep together!

World 2

Prime factors: one tool for both

Break each number into prime bricks (2, 3, 5, 7, …). Then the bricks tell you everything.

⚡
PGCD = the bricks they share. PPCM = all the bricks.Shared bricks go in the middle of the picture.
🫧 Lab 1 · The prime bubbles
🦊
Pick two numbers. Their prime bricks fly into two bubbles. Bricks both numbers have sit in the middle.
🪜 Lab 2 · The divide-down ladder (works for 3 numbers too)
🦊
This is the method from your sheet: divide by a prime, again and again. PPCM: divide the numbers it fits, until everything is 1. PGCD: only divide when the prime fits ALL the numbers, until none fits.

World 3

Sharing equally & the PGCD

PGCD = Plus Grand Commun Diviseur — the biggest number that divides them all.

⚡
PGCD = “share equally, as many groups as possible”.Nothing left over? The number of groups divides every amount.
📜 Sem-6 · Exercice 2 (2)Hard

🎒 Julien has 48 red, 64 blue and 112 green marbles. He wants the most bags possible, each with the same number of each colour, and no marble left over.

Show the exact wording
Julien veut mettre des billes dans de petits sacs. Il possède 48 billes rouges, 64 billes bleues et 112 billes vertes. Il veut faire le plus grand nombre possible de sacs contenant chacun le même nombre de billes de chaque couleur. Combien remplit-il de sacs s'il ne reste aucune bille ? Combien de billes retrouvera-t-on dans chaque sac ?

The point to land

Break numbers into prime bricks. Then the two big ideas are just two ways of picking bricks.

  • PPCM → all the bricks, highest power
  • PGCD → only shared bricks, lowest power
  • Check → PPCM × PGCD = a × b (two numbers)
  • Story → “again” = PPCM · “share equally” = PGCD
