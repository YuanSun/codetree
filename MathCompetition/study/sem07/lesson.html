World 4

La subordonnée circonstancielle

Grammar · which kind of proposition is it, and which mood follows the conjunction?

⚡
Three kinds of subordinate clauses — three tests.Relative → describes a noun (qui, que, dont, duquel…). Complétive → can be replaced by ça (je crois que…). Circonstancielle → starts with a conjunction of time, purpose, cause… (la subordonnée circonstancielle).
🔍 Play · Clause detective
🦊
Ten sentences from the sheet. The bold part is the clause. Which type is it? Use the three tests!
🎛️ Play · Indicatif or subjonctif?
🦊
Each conjunction commands a mood. Sort 12 of them into indicatif, subjonctif, or les deux (the meaning decides).

The point to land

Prime bricks, total = mean × count, “of” = ×, and a clause has a job.

  • PPCM → every prime, biggest power · PGCD → shared primes, smallest power
  • Mean → mean × how many = total
  • Fractions → “of” means multiply
  • Clauses → noun → relative · ça → complétive · conjunction → circonstancielle
