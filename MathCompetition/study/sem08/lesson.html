World 4

Tout

Grammar · déterminant, pronom, adverbe.

⚡
Tout = a chameleon: determiner, pronoun or adverb.Déterminant (before a noun) → agrees with the noun. Pronom (before a verb) → agrees with what it stands for. Adverbe (before an adjective) → invariable, except before a feminine adjective starting with a consonne or h aspiré.

The point to land

Watch the signs, read the brackets, and ask what tout is doing.

  • Signs → same → + · different → − · count the negatives
  • Powers → (−5)² = 25 · −5² = −25
  • Years → end − start
  • Tout → noun → agrees · verb → agrees · adjective → mostly invariable
