Geneva: Reports, Recs & Troubleshooting

Geneva is only as useful as your ability to ask it the right question. This module is a reference: which report answers which accounting question, and how to diagnose a break.

Report names vary. The categories below are the standard ones in portfolio accounting systems and exist in Geneva in some form (standard, Report Writer-based, or custom). Look up the actual names in your own report library.

Report parameters that bite

  • Period start/end (effective date) and knowledge date.
  • Portfolio vs group: is the report consolidating?
  • Basis (e.g. book vs tax) and currency (local vs base).
  • Trade-date vs settle-date view: critical for broker reconciliations.
  • Include/exclude pending or unsettled items, closed positions, zero balances.

Reconciling Geneva to the outside world

Positions

  1. Run the Geneva holdings on the same date and basis (settle-date for custodian comparison; trade-date for broker trade positions).
  2. Match by identifier (CUSIP/ISIN/SEDOL/FIGI) and account; mismatched identifiers create false breaks.
  3. Explain remaining differences: unbooked trade, unprocessed corporate action, free-of-payment transfers, short vs long sign.

Cash

  1. Compare by currency and account on value date.
  2. Look for: missing fees and interest, FX settlement legs, margin movements, dividends and withholding differences.

Ledger vs administrator or manager books

Compare trial balances. Differences usually trace to classification (realized vs unrealized), accrual timing, or different treatment of fees.

Method. Bisect. Is the difference in quantity, price, FX, or accrual? Which date does it first appear? Which transaction's knowledge date is after your last release? Narrowing the where-and-when almost always names the cause.

Report Writer and custom reporting

Geneva includes a report-writing capability to build or modify layouts, plus data export for analysis in Excel/BI. Senior habits:

  • Never overwrite a production report; copy, change, test against a known period, then promote under change control.
  • Document the logic of every custom column (what's the source, what's excluded).
  • Build a few validation reports whose only job is to catch breaks (e.g., positions with no price, flows not allocated, unbalanced investor capital).
